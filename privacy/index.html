Privacy

Privacy is a product requirement for Three, not an afterthought or a marketing claim.

Three stores the information needed to run the task list on your devices. It does not use ads, analytics, third-party tracking, or developer-operated servers for your task data.

Effective date

May 1, 2026

Data stored by the app

  • Task title
  • Important and urgent flags
  • Task status
  • Focus selection and focus position state
  • Completion state and timestamps required for app behavior
  • Small app preferences, such as onboarding and display settings

Where task data is stored

  • Task data is stored on your device.
  • If iCloud is available, task data syncs through the user's private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service.
  • The developer does not operate a task-data server and does not have access to the contents of your tasks.
  • No separate Three account is required.

Data not collected

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics or telemetry events
  • Contact lists
  • Location data
  • Browsing history
  • Health or financial data
  • Behavioral tracking

Retention and deletion

  • Active tasks remain until you edit, complete, or delete them.
  • Completed tasks are retained for 14 days, then permanently removed automatically.
  • Deleting the app removes local app data from that device. iCloud-synced data may remain available through your iCloud account according to Apple's iCloud behavior and settings.
  • Because the developer does not have access to your task data, deletion requests for task contents must be handled on your device or through your Apple/iCloud account controls.

Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives the information you choose to include in that message, such as your email address, device details, screenshots, or issue description. Support email is used only to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords, sensitive personal information, or private task contents unless you decide they are necessary for support.

Third parties

Three uses Apple platform services, including local device storage and iCloud/CloudKit sync. Three does not include third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, data brokers, or tracking services. If future versions add a third-party service that can access user data, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Privacy commitments

  • No ads
  • No third-party tracking
  • No analytics SDKs
  • No unnecessary user data collection
  • No developer access to task contents

Privacy questions can be sent to apps@materialandmethod.com.
