Privacy

Three

Home

Three is designed to collect no unnecessary user data. The app is intended to help users focus on today's work without advertising, tracking, or invasive analytics.

Data used by the app

  • Task title
  • Important and urgent flags
  • Task status
  • Focus selection and focus position state
  • Completion state and timestamps needed for app behavior

Data not collected

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contact lists
  • Location data
  • Browsing history
  • Health or financial data
  • Behavioral tracking unrelated to core functionality

Storage and retention

  • Task data is intended to sync through iCloud across the user's devices.
  • No separate account system is planned.
  • Completed tasks are intended to be retained for 14 days and then permanently removed automatically.
