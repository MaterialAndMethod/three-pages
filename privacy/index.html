Privacy

Privacy is a product requirement for Three, not an afterthought or a marketing claim.

Three is intended to collect only the information required to operate the app, without advertising, third-party tracking, or unnecessary behavioral profiling.

Data used by the app

  • Task title
  • Important and urgent flags
  • Task status
  • Focus selection and focus position state
  • Completion state and timestamps required for app behavior

Data not collected

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contact lists
  • Location data
  • Browsing history
  • Health or financial data
  • Behavioral tracking unrelated to core functionality

Storage and retention

  • Task data is intended to sync through iCloud across the user's devices.
  • No separate account system is planned.
  • Completed tasks are intended to be retained for 14 days, then permanently removed automatically.

Privacy commitments

  • No ads
  • No third-party tracking
  • No unnecessary user data collection
  • Use platform services where possible

Privacy questions can be sent to apps@materialandmethod.com.
