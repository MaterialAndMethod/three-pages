Support

Reach out for help with bugs, sync questions, App Store issues, or general product feedback.

Support for Three is handled directly. If you run into a problem, include a short description and any details that make the issue easier to reproduce.

Contact

apps@materialandmethod.com

Ben Wilson handles support for Three. Email support only receives the information you choose to include.

Support topics

  • Bug reports and unexpected behavior
  • Questions about privacy and data handling
  • iCloud sync questions
  • App Store purchase or availability questions

What to include

  • Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version
  • A short description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce it, if possible
  • Screenshots or context that clarify what happened
  • No passwords or sensitive personal information

Three stays small, clear, and direct, so support is handled the same way.
