Core Ideas

Built to feel clear, calm, and immediate.

01

Prioritize by important and urgent

Tasks are organized around what matters most instead of turning the app into a scheduling system or a long-term archive.

02

Choose just three for today

Daily focus is limited on purpose so the product encourages completion, not task accumulation or productivity theater.

03

Keep history short and useful

Completed work is kept for a limited window, then removed automatically so the experience stays oriented around the present.
