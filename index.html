Working title

Three

A private, focused to do list app built around choosing and finishing three meaningful tasks each day.

Support Privacy

Built to help you move forward

Three is designed for people who feel overwhelmed by traditional task managers. Instead of pushing endless list accumulation, it helps you prioritize by importance and urgency, then commit to just three tasks for today.

Focus over accumulation

Choose three meaningful tasks for today and keep attention on finishing, not organizing.

Private by design

No ads, no tracking, no unnecessary data collection. iCloud sync keeps data with the user.

Built for iPhone and iPad

A native Apple experience designed for portrait and landscape use across supported devices.
